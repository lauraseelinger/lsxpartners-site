Agencies are in a tough spot right now. Teams are at capacity executing content and don't have the time or resources to integrate AI visibility. Mastering a new expertise in-house either requires a new FT hire, or countless unbillable hours of research. But when the longtime client asks the dreaded question about where they stand in ChatGPT or another AI's search, agencies have to make the call — do you keep up the charade or do you bring in someone who already knows the AI visibility landscape.

I do this for agencies across industries — B2C, B2B, healthcare, nonprofit — so the process below is the same whether your client is in CPG, software sales or medical technology.

## When should an agency bring in an AI visibility consultant?

It usually starts with the client asking something the team can't answer. Why are we invisible in ChatGPT when we're number one on Google? Why is our traffic sliding when our rankings are holding? ([That second one has a specific answer.](/blog/traffic-down-zero-click-search/)) Other times it's a redesign, and someone finally realizes the new site has to be built for AI to retrieve it, not just for humans and Google.

You can absolutely build this capability in-house, and some agencies should. Whether it's your own team, [your existing SEO partner](/blog/ai-visibility-consultant-vs-seo-agency/), or a dedicated specialist is its own call. But I don't recommend white-knuckling it. AI visibility is new, and plenty of agencies are fumbling through it the same way brands are — no shame in it, it's just moving faster than most teams can keep up with, especially when the learning eats unbillable hours. Bringing in a specialist is the faster path to a confident answer, and it spares your team months of trial and error.

## What does an AI visibility consultant actually do?

As an AI visibility consultant working with agencies, I don't just provide a dashboard and list of recommendations. I set up a process that first creates a brand and industry-specific AI visibility baseline, and then provides specific optimizations and content topics — with specific definition blocks, H2s, etc. — to position the brand in AI search. And since AI visibility is a newer specialty for marketers, the brands and agencies who invest in this first will enjoy a significant head start as their competition kicks the can down the road.

The step I see most teams missing is the audience intelligence piece. Buyers using AI search are describing their situation or problem, not a short keyword phrase. A brand ranking for a high-volume keyword could be completely absent in AI if the user is typing in a specific situation they don't address in their content. These insights go deeper than audience personas. Figuring out what questions their audience is actually asking — the raw material for the prompt audit — is exactly what the [audience intelligence process](/blog/ai-intelligence-loop/) is for.

## How does working with an embedded fractional partner work?

As an embedded agency partner, I engage with the team the way a senior strategist would — active in your working docs and internal calls, and prepping with you before the client presentation. When a client requires AI visibility judgment, I handle the research and the strategy, and you get a monthly synthesis rather than a specialist you have to manage day to day. And if a client has specific questions related to their visibility or the process, I am on hand to join the call and explain things directly to them. This takes significant weight off the agency leads responsible for client relationships.

While I am a contracted specialist, there is an option for client deliverables to be white-labeled under the agency's brand.

## Does my agency need a one-time project or an ongoing retainer?

It depends what your client roster or agency requires. Agencies often start off with single project engagements, such as an AI visibility blueprint to set the foundation for a client. This includes a strategic assessment of the brand's identity across AI, as well as an in-depth prompt audit. The blueprint deliverable provides the agency with the findings, recommendations and ready-to-implement page templates for content and structural updates ensuring optimal AI retrievability.

A client retainer starts at the same point as the single engagement, but it sets the client up for ongoing monitoring. The blueprint serves as the AI visibility baseline, informing on-site and third-party content strategies. Retainer clients receive a monthly report of the brand's AI visibility within their category (Share of Voice, answer mentions, citations), along with strategic recommendations and prioritizations for the next round of content.

## How do you keep the client relationship clean?

The agency owns the client relationship and makes the decision whether to white-label services or not. There is even an option for me to engage with your clients using an agency domain email address.

## What should an agency look for when hiring an AI visibility consultant?

A few things separate a partner worth hiring from someone selling you a dashboard login.

Start with honesty about certainty. Nobody can guarantee your client gets cited by AI. The engines generate their answers first and attach citations afterward, so the real work raises the probability of showing up, it doesn't promise it. Anyone selling guaranteed citations is selling something the industry doesn't have, and that's your first tell.

Then look for real audience intelligence behind the recommendations, not a tool's output reworded into a slide. That research is the part that's hard to fake and hard to commoditize. The framework wrapped around it is not.

Measurement matters too, across more than one tool, because different tools answer different questions. One gauges how AI perceives the brand against its competitors, another checks how accurately AI describes it. Lean on a single score and you get a single, flattering story.

And circle back to the relationship. Hire the partner who's trying to make your agency look brilliant to its client, not the one using your account to build their own brand.
